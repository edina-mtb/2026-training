# 🟢 May Training Plan — High School MTB (Pre-Season Base)

## Month Snapshot

- **Dates:** May 1–31, 2026
- **Phase:** Pre-Season Base
- **Secondary Overlay:** Early Build Introduction
- **Primary Focus:** Aerobic base, consistent rhythm, early skills, controlled intensity

---

## 🎯 Primary Objectives

- Build aerobic base and time on the bike
- Establish a repeatable weekly training rhythm
- Introduce skills work early while riders are fresh
- Keep intensity purposeful and limited
- Support 500 Mile Club participation through steady volume

---

## 📅 Standard Weekly Rhythm

- **Monday — Off-Bike Strength**
- **Tuesday — Hard Effort 1**
- **Wednesday — Easy Trail Ride**
- **Thursday — Hard Effort 2**
- **Friday — Skills / Flow**
- **Saturday — Endurance**
- **Sunday — Total Rest**

### Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced Adjustments

- **Beginner:** shorten the Tuesday and Thursday hard efforts, keep Saturday closer to 1 hour, and keep Wednesday/Friday very relaxed
- **Intermediate:** follow the standard structure as written, with Saturday usually in the middle of the endurance range
- **Advanced:** keep the same weekly pattern but extend Saturday toward 2 hours and add slightly more work inside Tuesday and Thursday sessions only if recovery stays good

### Spring Sport Athletes

Prioritize:
- 1 endurance ride/week
- 1 short skills ride
- basic mobility/core

De-emphasize:
- extra interval work
- extra volume
- multiple hard efforts in one week

---

## 🗓️ Week-by-Week Playbook

### Week 1 — May 1–3
Focus:
- start conservatively
- re-establish riding rhythm
- begin accumulating easy miles

Structure:
- 1 endurance-oriented ride
- 1 short skills session if time allows
- no need to force a full interval day in the opening partial week

### Week 2 — May 4–10
Focus:
- settle into the full weekly rhythm
- introduce the first structured intensity

Structure:
- Monday off-bike strength
- Tuesday short, fast hills or sprints
- Wednesday easy trail ride with one specific skill focus
- Thursday longer steady-hard race-simulation effort
- Friday easy flow ride with cornering practice
- Saturday endurance ride
- Sunday full rest

### Week 3 — May 11–17
Focus:
- maintain rhythm
- use the River Falls XC option selectively

Structure:
- if not racing: complete the normal Monday-through-Sunday structure
- if racing River Falls on May 17: use it as the week’s Thursday-style race-simulation effort and reduce surrounding intensity

### Week 4 — May 18–24
Focus:
- recover from any race effort
- resume mostly aerobic work

Structure:
- return to the standard structure if recovery is good
- shorten Tuesday or Thursday if race fatigue is still lingering
- keep Friday easy and Saturday aerobic

### Week 5 — May 25–31
Focus:
- finish the month with consistency rather than forcing extra load
- use Chisholm XC selectively

Structure:
- if not racing: complete the normal Monday-through-Sunday structure
- if racing Chisholm on May 31: use it as the week’s Thursday-style hard effort and keep the surrounding days easier

## 🏁 Race Integration This Month

### Relevant Events

- **May 17 — River Falls XC**
  - Type: XC simulation
  - Use: replaces the interval / race-simulation slot

- **May 31 — Chisholm XC**
  - Type: XC simulation
  - Use: replaces the interval / race-simulation slot

### Race Rules

- Do not stack an interval day on top of a race week
- If an athlete skips one of the XC simulation races and that race was intended as the week’s hard effort, replace it with an interval day
- If no race is used that weekend, default to an endurance ride
- Weekend races should be treated as training, not as a peak performance target

---

## 🚴 Key Workouts

### 1. Interval Day
Purpose:
- introduce controlled intensity

Example:
- 6 x 2–3 min moderate-hard efforts
- full recovery between efforts

May Pattern:
- Tuesday = short, fast hills or sprint efforts for VO2 max
- Thursday = longer steady-hard efforts for race simulation

### 2. Easy Ride + Skills Day
Focus:
- cornering
- braking
- climbing technique
- descending

May Pattern:
- Wednesday = easy trail ride with one specific skill focus
- Friday = easy flow ride focused on enjoying the trail and practicing cornering

### 3. Endurance Ride

- **Beginner:** 1.0 hour
- **Intermediate:** 1.25–1.75 hours
- **Advanced:** 1.75–2.0 hours
- **Intensity:** zone 2 / conversational pace

### 4. Strength Training

- core
- mobility
- bodyweight strength
- bodyweight squats
- planks
- stretching

---

## 🔁 Weekend Substitution Rules

- **Missed XC simulation race:** replace with interval day if the race was meant to be the hard effort
- **Race on a weekend:** use the race in place of the Thursday race-simulation slot and keep Saturday aerobic if needed
- **No weekend event selected:** default to endurance ride
- **Heavy fatigue week:** keep the endurance ride shorter rather than forcing intensity

---

## 🧠 Coaching Guidelines

- 80–90% easy riding
- Avoid stacking intensity
- Consistency over volume spikes
- Skills belong on Wednesday and Friday when the riding stays easy
- Leave the month feeling ready for more, not cooked

---

## ✅ Weekly Checklist

- Monday off-bike strength completed
- Tuesday VO2 max hills or sprints completed
- Wednesday easy trail ride with one skill focus completed
- Thursday race-simulation effort completed
- Friday flow / cornering ride completed
- Saturday endurance ride completed
- Sunday total rest completed
