# 🟠 August Training Plan — High School MTB (Sharpen)

## Month Snapshot

- **Dates:** August 1–31, 2026
- **Phase:** Sharpen
- **Secondary Overlay:** Race Season begins
- **Primary Focus:** increase specificity, protect freshness, and arrive ready for the first A-races

---

## 🎯 Primary Objectives

- Transition from general summer fitness to race-specific readiness
- Keep weekday work sharp but not excessive
- Use optional events only when they support the A-races
- Reduce junk volume and non-purposeful fatigue
- Start the A-race block feeling eager, not flat

---

## 📅 Standard Weekly Rhythm

- Team practices are the primary weekday quality / skills sessions
- Weekend focus alternates between selective racing and endurance
- Strength stays minimal and maintenance-oriented
- Extra volume is used sparingly

### Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced Adjustments

- **Beginner:** prioritize practices and required races, keep optional racing limited
- **Intermediate:** use one selective optional event if it clearly supports readiness
- **Advanced:** may use more optional events, but only when recovery and A-race quality stay protected

## 🚴 Key Workouts

### 1. Practice / Midweek Sharpening
Purpose:
- short, race-relevant intensity
- handling under pressure

### 2. Race-Simulation XC Effort
Purpose:
- rehearse pacing, starts, and sustained hard work

### 3. Endurance Ride

- **Beginner:** 75–120 min
- **Intermediate:** 90–150 min
- **Advanced:** 2–3 hours
- enough to maintain aerobic support without blunting sharpness

### 4. Strength Maintenance

- 0–1 light sessions per week
- mobility and activation preferred over fatigue-producing work

---

## 🔁 Weekend Substitution Rules

- **Missed optional XC race:** replace with interval day only if the week still needs race-simulation work
- **Missed endurance-oriented weekend:** replace with endurance ride
- **No weekend event:** default to endurance
- **A-race week:** skip substitutions that add unnecessary fatigue

---

## 🧠 Coaching Guidelines

- Sharpen, do not bury
- Optional events are tools, not obligations
- A-races are now the organizing force of the month
- Freshness matters more than squeezing in one more hard day

---

## ✅ Weekly Checklist

- weekday hard work stayed selective
- optional events supported A-races rather than competed with them
- endurance remained present but controlled
- recovery improved as races became more important
