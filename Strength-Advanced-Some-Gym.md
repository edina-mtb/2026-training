MOBILITY/STRECHING ROUTINE: Do each movement slowly and with control.
Breathe deeply and focus on quality over quantity. This should take about 10 minutes.
World’s Greatest Stretch – 30 sec per side - Opens hips, hamstrings and upper back
1. Get in deep lunge position with right leg forward and back leg straight. Squeeze your glut
muscles hard and you should feel a stretch in the hip.
2. 3. Push your forward knee out to the side for added stretch of the hip.
Then place right elbow to right foot instep on ground and rotate arm, trunk and head out and
up reaching to the sky.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-CiWQ2IvY34
Cat-Cow + Thread the Needle – 1 min total – Mobilizes spine and shoulders
1. Cat-Cow: Start on all fours in a tabletop position. Inhale as you arch your back, lifting your
head and tailbone (Cow Pose). Exhale as you round your spine, tucking your chin and pelvis (Cat
Pose). Repeat for 30 seconds
2. Thread the Needle: From the tabletop position, lean back into your heals and slide your right
arm under your left arm, allowing your right shoulder and ear to rest on the floor, then rotate out
and up towards the sky. Hold 15 seconds on floor and sky, switch sides.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cU87KDJBUyM
Deep Squat Hold – 1 min total – Targets hips, ankles and lower back
1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart. Lower into a deep squat, keeping your heels on the
ground and chest upright.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/KAezSfVOksc
Video Example Rocking: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/JZpqxQbe9N4
Downward Dog to Cobra Flow – 5 reps – Targets hamstrings, calves, spine and chest
1. Begin in a plank position. Push your hips up and back into Downward Dog, keeping your
heels aiming toward the floor.
2. Then, shift forward, lowering your hips and lifting your chest into Cobra Pose.
3. Alternate between these two positions for five repetitions.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3x0CjmxEb08
Hamstring Sweep/Toe Swipe – 10 reps – Targets hamstrings and calves
1. Stand tall and extend your right leg forward, resting your heel on the ground with toes
pointing up. Hinge at the hips and sweep your hands toward your toes. Return to standing and
switch legs. Repeat for 10 reps on each side
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/JLsv09708zs
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jG1DPrIsDn4

2. Next swing arms forward and backwards leading with your thumbs on way up and reaching
