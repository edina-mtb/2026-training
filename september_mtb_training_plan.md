# 🔴 September Training Plan — High School MTB (Race Season / Maintain)

## Month Snapshot

- **Dates:** September 1–30, 2026
- **Phase:** Race Season
- **Primary Focus:** maintain fitness, protect recovery, and perform well on required race weekends

---

## 🎯 Primary Objectives

- Keep required race weekends at the center of planning
- Maintain aerobic fitness without unnecessary fatigue
- Use weekday quality sparingly and only when it supports the next race
- Recover well between required races
- Avoid optional racing that compromises the main goals

---

## 📅 Standard Weekly Rhythm

- Team practices remain the main weekday structure
- Required races dictate the week when present
- Non-race weekends default to endurance unless a selective optional race fits cleanly
- Strength stays minimal and maintenance-only

### Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced Adjustments

- **Beginner:** simplify around required races, emphasize recovery and confidence
- **Intermediate:** maintain routine, but cut extra volume around A-races
- **Advanced:** may retain slightly more volume, but must still protect race readiness
