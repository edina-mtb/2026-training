Key notes:
-For weight/Intensity, choose a weight that you can lift with controlled & steady form as guided below.
-Slowly increase weight as you progress from week to week.
-You should feel like you have 2-4 more reps left when you complete a set.
-all exercises can be done weighted with dumbbells or kettlebell, whichever you have access to works
-If you don’t have access to weights, try to do the exercises explosively/fast while still being
controlled.
LOWER BODY EXERCISES: each week as you get comfortable with the exercise
Start with light weight kettlebell or dumbbells and increase
Lateral Lunges - 3 sets of 10 reps, 5 on each side (left and right), 2 minutes between sets
1. 2. Hold a dumbbell in each hand with palms facing inward.
Take one large step laterally (to the side) as you lower with your lead leg and keep the
dumbbells on either side of your lead foot. Make sure you keep your chest up and back flat.
3. 4. Press your feet through the floor and rise back up to standing.
Finish all the reps on one leg, then switch legs and repeat.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R8jArZG2J6Q
Goblet Squats – 3 sets x 10 reps, 2 minutes between sets
5. 6. 7. Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell close to your chest.
Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly turned out.
Slowly lower your body into a deep squat, keeping your chest up and knees tracking over your
toes.
8. Push through your heels to return to standing as fast as you can.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MeIiIdhvXT4&t=4s
Forward Lunges - 3 sets x 10 reps per leg, 2 minutes between sets
1. With a shoulder width stance hold 2 dumbbells at your sides with palms facing your sides/
inward.
2. Take one step forward with one leg, then bend your knees to lower your body towards the
ground until your front thigh is parallel to the ground and back knee is just hovering above the
ground. Ensure your front shin is perpendicular to the ground. Do not allow front knee to lean
past your toes.
3. Press your feet through the ground to push back to a standing position. Ensure shoulders are
pulled back, chest is up and core is engaged throughout entire movement.
4. Continue all the reps with one leg and then switch legs.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3TM-vVWuLYE
Jump Squats - 3 sets x 8 reps, 2 minutes between sets
1. Grasp a dumbbell in each hand and let your arms hang down by your sides with your palms
facing your thighs in a neutral grip.
2. Position your feet in a slightly wider than shoulder width stance with your toes slightly pointed
out and knees slightly bent. Keep your neck neutral and maintain a straight back.
3. Descend into a squat by pushing your hips and butt backwards – continue down until your
upper legs are parallel to the floor.
4. Once you reach the bottom position, powerfully push through your heels to jump upwards
keeping your arms fully extended by your side and land on the balls of your feet.
5. Immediately propel yourself upwards for another repetition & repeat.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XOTO2qWRy9U
UPPER BODY EXERCISES: week as you get comfortable with the exercise
Start with light weight kettlebell or dumbbells and increase each
Standing Shoulder Press - 3 sets x 10 reps, 2 minutes between sets
1. Hold dumbbells at shoulder height with bent arms rotating out about 45 degree angle with feet
slightly wider than shoulder width stance, slightly bent knees and engage your core.
2. Press the weights over the head and slowly lower them back to the starting position with
dumbbells right by your ears.
3. Repeat.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/wO0l5jW2NtQ
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OOe_HrNnQWw
Dumbbell Bent-Over Rows - 3 sets x 8 reps, 2 minutes between sets
1. 2. 3. Hinge at the hips with a straight back and soft knees – drop arms and hold dumbbells which
should fall right below your knees.
Tuck your chin and Pull the weights up driving your elbows up toward the ceiling while
squeezing your shoulder blades.
Slowly lower back down & repeat.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/IOOLhrkN_NI
Push-Ups - 3 sets x 12 reps, 2 minutes between sets
1. 2. 3. 4. Start in a plank position with hands slightly wider than shoulder-width.
Lower your body until your chest nearly touches the floor.
Keep your elbows at a 45-degree angle.
Push back up to the starting position.
Video Link: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ba8tr1NzwXU
Video Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e22iFFXY8vw
Farmer’s Carry - 3 sets x 30 seconds, 2 minutes between sets
1. 2. 3. Pickup heavy dumbbells with arms down at sides.
Squeeze/grip the weights 25% to create tension up the arms.
Roll your shoulders back and lock them in place.
4. Walk around back and forth across the room, slow and controlled.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z7E_YU9P1jU
CORE EXERCISES: Find a comfortable spot to do these on the floor
Plank - 3 x 30-60 seconds, 1 minute between sets
1. Get into a forearm plank position with shoulders over the elbows, placing your palms on the
ground. Grip the floor with your palms and keep your head facing down.
2. Keep your back straight and core tight and begin to pull your elbows down – you should feel
your core muscles activating.
3. Fully straighten your legs by squeezing your thighs & squeeze glutes to tuck your butt in while
maintaining a flat back.
4. Hold for 30-60 seconds.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/v25dawSzRTM
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yeKv5oX_6GY
Russian Twists - 2 sets x 30 seconds, 1 minute between sets
1. Sit on the floor with knees bent and heels on the floor. Lean back slightly to 45 degree angle
with your arms straight out from your chest and your back perfectly straight.
2. Slowly lift both heels off the floor while balancing on your tailbone and twist your torso,
rotating your arms and chest from left to right as far as you can. (You can keep your heels on
the floor if needed and lift when you feel strong enough)
3. 4. Continue until you’ve completed all of your repetitions.
You can add a weight if desired.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lvNPZCtt8NQ
Flutter Kicks - 3 x 30 seconds, 1 minute between sets
1. Lay down on your back on the ground and tilt your pelvis back so there is no gap between your
lower back and the floor. (cannot slide your hand under your back)
2. Next raise your legs straight overhead together and then lower them while keeping your back
completely flat on the ground.
3. Brace your arms on the ground and move your legs back and forth in a flutter kick motion. It
does not matter how fast you move your legs. Do this for 30 seconds and then rest and repeat.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ZloeWSzQtTA
Dead Bug - 2 sets x 20 seconds, 1 minute between sets
1. Lie on your back with arms and legs raised with your knees over your hips, tilt pelvis back so
your back is pressed into the ground and there is no gap between your back and the floor.
2. Point your arms straight up so they are pointing at the ceiling. From this position, fully extend
your left leg with toes continuing to point at the ceiling and lower back maintaining contact
with the ground. Reset to the starting position and switch sides to extend other leg and
continue alternating leg extensions.
3. As you become comfortable with this exercise, add the arms, extending one arm over your head
towards the ground simultaneously with one leg extending at the same time (can be same or
opposites) while keeping your back flat.
Video Instructions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MCVX9wRd_h0
