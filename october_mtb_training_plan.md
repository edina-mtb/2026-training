# 🏁 October Training Plan — High School MTB (Peak)

## Month Snapshot

- **Dates:** October 1–31, 2026
- **Phase:** Peak
- **Primary Focus:** reduce non-essential fatigue, stay sharp, and peak for State Championship

---

## 🎯 Primary Objectives

- Arrive fresh and confident for State Championship
- Keep sharpness without creating lingering fatigue
- Taper volume while preserving rhythm
- Recover well after State
- Close the season in a healthy, positive place

---

## 📅 Standard Weekly Rhythm

- Short, purposeful quality
- Lower overall volume
- No unnecessary optional racing
- Recovery and freshness prioritized over load

### Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced Adjustments

- **Beginner:** keep the taper simple, focus on confidence and freshness
- **Intermediate:** maintain some short sharpening efforts, reduce total volume
- **Advanced:** preserve rhythm with carefully controlled intensity, but do not overload

---

## 🗓️ Week-by-Week Playbook

### Week 1 — October 1–4
Focus:
- begin reducing unnecessary load
- keep some pop in the legs

Structure:
- one short sharpening effort
- one easy skills touch
- moderate endurance, not long fatigue

### Week 2 — October 5–11
Focus:
- State week

Structure:
- short, crisp efforts early in the week
- more recovery than work as the race approaches
- Saturday, October 11 should be used for openers, bike shakeout, and confirming there are no mechanical issues
- optional course inspection at Redhead may be useful if the rider is willing to travel
- Sunday, October 12 is the actual State Championship race day and the full focus

### Week 3 — October 12–18
Focus:
- recover physically and mentally after State

Structure:
- easy spins
- optional very light skills or fun riding
- no structured hard work needed immediately after State

### Week 4 — October 19–25
Focus:
- transition out of the peak
- maintain activity without pressure

Structure:
- mostly easy, enjoyable riding
- mobility and recovery work

### Week 5 — October 26–31
Focus:
- close the season well
- reset for off-season transition

Structure:
- unstructured easy riding
- reflect on the season and identify lessons

---

## 🏁 Race Integration This Month

### Relevant Events

- **October 11–12 — State Championship**
  - Type: required A-race / peak event
  - Use: Saturday is for openers and bike check, Sunday is the race; all training serves readiness for this weekend

### Race Rules

- No optional races should interfere with State preparation
- Sharpening workouts are short and controlled
- Volume should taper down as State approaches
- After State, there is no need to “make up” missed training

---

## 🚴 Key Workouts

### 1. Sharpening Session
Purpose:
- maintain race feel without accumulating fatigue

Example:
- 3–4 short race-pace efforts
- full recovery

### 2. Easy Skills Session
Purpose:
- confidence
- handling smoothness
- staying connected to the bike

### 3. Light Endurance Ride

- **Beginner:** 60–75 min
- **Intermediate:** 75–105 min
- **Advanced:** 90–120 min
- keep it aerobic and finish feeling fresh

---

## 🔁 Weekend Substitution Rules

- **Before State:** no replacement hard work is needed for skipped optional activities; default to easy aerobic riding
- **State weekend:** this is the event the month is built around
- **After State:** recovery and transition take precedence over substitution logic

---

## 🧠 Coaching Guidelines

- Peak through freshness, not through extra work
- Sharpening should create confidence, not fatigue
- Taper volume, not motivation
- After State, shift into recovery and healthy transition

---

## ✅ Weekly Checklist

- training volume was reduced appropriately
- one short sharpening stimulus was enough
- State week felt calm and controlled
- post-State recovery was respected
