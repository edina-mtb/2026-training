# 🟡 July Training Plan — High School MTB (Base to Team Practice Transition)

## Month Snapshot

- **Dates:** July 1–31, 2026
- **Phase:** Pre-Season Base
- **Secondary Overlay:** Build
- **Primary Focus:** transition from individual summer structure into team-practice rhythm after July 7

---

## 🎯 Primary Objectives

- Maintain aerobic foundation while team practices begin
- Let team practices replace most weekday quality work
- Use optional races selectively around practice load
- Continue accumulating endurance and trail time
- Arrive at August ready to sharpen, not fatigued

---

## 📅 Standard Weekly Rhythm

### Before July 7

- **1 interval day**
- **1 easy ride + skills day**
- **1 endurance ride**
- **1–2 strength sessions**

### After July 7

- **Team practices replace most weekday quality and skills work**
- **1 key weekend endurance ride or race**
- **Optional extra easy recovery volume only as needed**
- **Strength becomes maintenance-oriented, not a major fatigue source**

### Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced Adjustments

- **Beginner:** prioritize attending practices, keep extra rides modest, default to endurance over extra racing
- **Intermediate:** standard structure with selective racing
- **Advanced:** may absorb slightly more total volume, but should still avoid stacking practice intensity and races

---

### Week 5 — July 27–31
Focus:
- absorb work

Structure:
- Buck Hill on July 30 can replace interval work if used
- keep volume steady, not excessive

### Race Rules

- After July 7, do not assume riders still need a separate weekday interval day if practice already provides that stimulus
- Midweek races replace weekday quality
- Weekend XC races replace the hard race-simulation slot
- If no weekend race is used, default to endurance
- If an XC race was intended as the hard effort and the rider skips it, replace it with intervals only if practice load does not already cover that stimulus

---

## 🚴 Key Workouts

### 1. Team Practice Quality Sessions
Purpose:
- become the main source of weekday intensity and structured skills after July 7

### 2. Short-Course Race / Intensity Session
Purpose:
- sharpen handling and repeated hard efforts

### 3. Weekend Endurance Ride

- **Beginner:** 90–120 min
- **Intermediate:** 2–3 hours
- **Advanced:** 3–4+ hours
- **Intensity:** mainly aerobic

### 4. Strength Maintenance

- 1 session most weeks
- mobility, core, durability
- avoid heavy lifting that interferes with practice quality

---

## 🔁 Weekend Substitution Rules

- **Missed XC simulation race:** replace with interval day only if team practice that week did not already provide equivalent quality
- **No weekend event selected:** default to endurance ride
- **Heavy practice week:** shorten the endurance ride rather than adding more hard work

---

## 🧠 Coaching Guidelines

- Team practices now matter more than self-added weekday intensity
- Do not stack practices, midweek races, and weekend races recklessly
- Use optional events as tools, not obligations
- Keep the transition into August feeling controlled

---

## ✅ Weekly Checklist

- weekday quality came from practice or one selective race, not both stacked carelessly
- 1 endurance ride or XC race
- appropriate recovery around practice load
- strength kept in maintenance mode
